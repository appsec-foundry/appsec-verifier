## Resolved: steps where a proof needs more than are left (formerly open decision 1, task 3)

Run 160 (12 steps, safe mode) ended inconclusive without a refusal or a cost cut: six source reads, the plan at step 7, two lists and the other account's read, a negative verdict refused at step 11 for three missing reads with one step left. The listed-record proof cites six requests, so from the plan on it needs at least eight steps; 155, 157 and 158 each used nine after their plan (157 used 13 steps, not 12). A flat 16 would have lengthened all 65 of 243 recorded variants that ended at the step limit, 33 of them without an accepted plan.

VER-015 now says that once a test plan is accepted, the variant keeps at least the steps its proof needs, the requests the proof cites plus three, past the attempts bound. The first accepted plan sets it (`proof_steps` in `agent/experiments.py`), a revised plan adds none, the request-action bound follows, and the plan's answer names every request its access-control proof cites (`needs`), as the proof feedback did. Checked against all 83 recorded variants with an accepted plan: every access-control form fits its count (listed record 9 of 9, own record 6 of 7, signed-in comparison 1 to 2 of 3); injection plans used up to 7 decisions after the plan within the old bound, for which three is only a floor. Only 7 recorded variants would have got more steps (1 to 5 each). Not known: whether naming the requests at the plan makes the agent skip the refused verdict; a run will show it.

## Resolved: model refusals and per-call cost shares (formerly open issue 2)

The step failures of 154, 156 and 159 were refusals, not the cost share. Replaying the recorded first step of 154 five times with the same instructions, schema, prompt and flags ($0.06) gave two refusals: Claude CLI 2.1.330 reported `subtype: success`, `is_error: true`, `stop_reason: refusal`, exit 1 and $0.0037, with the text "Sonnet 5.5's safeguards flagged this session" and `Details: [cyber]`. The model had already given its structured answer, which the CLI drops on a refusal. `host_claude.py` read only `error_*` subtypes, so it reported `host_error (exit 1)`, a passing failure, and tried the call again. In the recorded runs the first step was repeated in 146, 152, 153, 154, 156, 158 and 159; the repetition succeeded in 153 and 158.

The cost share only stopped the technical QA that followed. Replaying the run's budget, the first step had the whole $30 (the third paid call), the repetition and the QA call $0.0347 each, and the QA call cost $0.039. Replaying the call costs of all 38 recorded runs (about 2 600 calls) with the share rule, only these three QA calls would have been cut, each in a run testing one finding.

Changes: a model's stop (`refusal`, `max_tokens`) is named alike for `claude` and `anthropic-api` (`model_refused`, `output_truncated`, `STOP_FAILURES` in `agent/hosts.py`) and, as VER-015 lists only host and server errors, timeouts and lost connections as passing, not tried again. The reason line says the model refused and suggests another model; a stopped technical QA names why on the console. A call takes a share only while another of the runner's workers can still test a finding (`RunBudget.add_testers`), so a run testing one finding at a time, and the last finding of a parallel run, are never cut by a share. Not covered: `codex` names a refusal `invalid_response` (also not repeated); what `codex-cli` and `copilot-cli` report for a refusal was not checked. Shares still apply while other workers wait for a dependency or for a finding that may write.

Open: whether a refusal should be tried again (VER-015) is a maintainer decision; the provider's message advises another model or a new session. Which model refuses less for these prompts is not measured.

## Analysis tasks

Each task names the problem, the evidence, where to look, and what the analysis should deliver. Per AGENTS.md, find the cause and fix it for every case with that cause, show offline that a proposed measure removes each observed symptom, and propose requirement wording before changing what a VER entry promises.

1. **Per-call cost shares end runs at random.** Resolved, see "Resolved: model refusals and per-call cost shares".
2. **Failed Claude CLI calls lose their reason.** Resolved for refusals, see the same section; other `host_error` failures still keep no CLI text.
3. **Step budget where a proof is close.** Resolved, see "Resolved: steps where a proof needs more than are left".
4. **Orientation before the plan costs most steps.** Evidence: T-010 read source for 3 to 7 steps before planning (151 to 158), mostly to understand `appendUserId`, although the access proof does not need it. Look at the access-control hint in `agent/prompts.py` and the step history of these runs. Deliver: whether a generic, cheaper instruction (plan first, read source only where the proof needs it) reduces orientation across classes, with the prompt length it adds.
5. **Technical QA asks for source reads that cost test steps.** Evidence: after early verdicts in 152 and 153 QA asked for the body of `appendUserId`; the agent spent 3 to 4 steps. Rounds with a follow-up appear in about 90 variant logs, often at steps 1 to 4. Look at `QA_SYSTEM` in `agent/technical_review.py` and where `qa.consider` runs in `agent/loop.py`. Deliver: how often QA questions lead to steps that no proof uses, and whether QA should ask for the requests the proof needs instead.
6. **Prerequisite reads count as proof.** Evidence: lists sent with `prerequisite: true` were accepted in 158; VER-047 says a prerequisite read is never a proof. No proof check looks at the flag. Look at `agent/proof.py` and the checks in `agent/experiments.py`. Deliver: whether every form should refuse such citations with the requests to cite, as for source reads, or whether VER-047 should allow a read that the plan itself permits, such as the collection GET of VER-067.
7. **Routes registered outside the finding's evidence cannot be derived.** Evidence: T-010 `POST /api/Cards` in `server.ts` while the evidence is `routes/payment.ts`; DVWA T-044 and T-045 refused with "the source is not the finding's evidence or a recorded local import from it" in 4 and 7 runs. Look at `_source_candidates` and `_source_roots` in `agent/derivation.py` and VER-008. Deliver: per case whether the file is related to the finding in a way code can check (registration, include, route table), and VER-008 wording if the rule should widen.
8. **Sessions some applications read from a cookie.** Evidence: Juice `/rest/user/whoami` answers `{"user":{}}` because `routes/currentUser.ts` reads `req.cookies.token` while the Verifier sends the token as a header; T-012 and T-037 fail for the same reason; agents then guess identifiers (149, 150 took basket 1, the admin's). Deliver: a generic rule for when a token login should also send the token as a cookie, without target-specific names, and its risk for other targets.
9. **Some records cannot be anchored to an owner.** Evidence: the Juice basket names only a numeric `UserId`, has no list, and the sign-in answer's `bid` is not bound to a collection; T-002 stays inconclusive. The list anchor reads JSON lists only, not an HTML "my orders" page. 10. **Negative comparison images show "Expected: not declared" for the attack.** Evidence: the 155 image; a negative verdict records no access restriction, so the attack row falls back to "not declared" although the run assumed one. Look at `comparison_summary` and the Expected column in `verify/poc.py`. Deliver: what the attack row should expect when the restriction is assumed and the verdict is negative.

Look at `finding_cost_fraction` and `min_finding_cost_usd` in `data/limits.yaml` and `check_finding_cost` in `limits.py`.
